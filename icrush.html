UX/UI + brand case study · Web3 social

ICRUSH

Designing a bright social identity that feels energetic without losing usability.

A playful brand and interface direction for a Web3 social product built around expression, personality, and fast recognition.

Energy vs clarity

The brand needed to feel loud and social while the interface stayed easy to understand.

Web3 trust

Users needed a more human entry into a category that can feel technical or cold.

Identity system

The visual language had to scale from logo and colors into product screens.

Research

The research focused on social energy, trust cues, and expressive digital identity.

Audience mood

Users respond to immediacy, color, humor, and self-expression.

Category tension

Web3 products need clarity and trust before users feel safe exploring.

Visual references

Social apps, creator tools, sticker culture, and high-contrast brand systems shaped the direction.

UX Strategy

Make the brand expressive, then make every action obvious.

Expressive first impression

Use color and motion to make the product feel alive.

Simple flows

Keep onboarding, profile, and interaction patterns familiar.

Trust through consistency

Repeat visual rules so the playful system still feels intentional.

Wireframes

Wireframes tested how much personality the interface could hold.

UI System

A sharp, colorful system designed for recognition.

Color

Warm pinks and contrast tones create a memorable social world.

Typography

Bold labels and clean body text balance personality and readability.

Components

Buttons, tags, cards, and profile elements reuse the same energetic rules.

Homepage Breakdown

The landing experience introduces a playful product world quickly.

Product Experience

The app experience should feel fast, expressive, and low-friction.

Social actions

Core actions stay familiar so the brand can be more expressive.

Identity moments

Profiles, badges, and reactions carry the personality of the product.

Motion

Motion adds spark without slowing the social loop.

Final Outcome

A social identity system that feels bright, memorable, and usable.

The final direction gives ICRUSH a high-energy brand language while keeping the product structure approachable.

Reflection

Playfulness works best when the rules underneath are simple.

Personality

Strong brand moments help the product stand out.

Usability

Familiar patterns keep the energy from becoming confusing.
