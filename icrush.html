Overview

A social product needs personality and trust at the same time.

The case explores how a Web3-adjacent social product can feel playful, expressive, and human while keeping its core actions clear.

Energy

A colorful system helps the brand feel memorable.

Trust

Familiar UX patterns reduce category anxiety.

Scale

The identity extends from logo to product screens.

My role

I connected brand expression with interface behavior.

My work focused on shaping the visual identity, defining key product moments, and making sure the expressive direction still supported practical flows.

  • Created a bright visual direction for brand recognition.
  • Structured product screens around familiar social interactions.
  • Balanced high-energy visuals with readable UI hierarchy.
Problem

Playful products can become confusing when the rules are unclear.

The challenge was making the brand feel loud and social without making the product feel chaotic. Web3 products also need extra trust cues because users may arrive with skepticism.

Category friction

Technical associations can make the product feel less approachable.

Visual intensity

Bright systems need strong layout rules to stay usable.

Solutions

Make expression feel easy to enter.

  • Build an identity that feels social, bold, and instantly recognizable.
  • Keep onboarding and interaction patterns familiar.
  • Use repeated component rules to make the brand feel intentional.
  • Design moments that can scale across product, social, and marketing.

Statement

The final direction gives ICRUSH a bold social identity while grounding its product experience in familiar, low-friction patterns.
