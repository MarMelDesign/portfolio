Visual identity / editorial mood / art direction

DE SOI

A romantic visual world built from softness, texture, quiet color, and intimate fragments.
01 / feeling

Design that behaves like a memory

DE SOI is shaped around a slow, cinematic mood: handwritten details, gentle contrast, warm product imagery, and compositions that feel collected instead of staged.

02 / system

Soft structure under the romance

The direction uses delicate spacing, tactile paper surfaces, muted editorial palettes, and small moments of surprise so the brand can expand across photo, social, motion, and web.

03 / motion

Video moments

Close-up texture loop

Slow macro shots for product surfaces, fruit, paper, and light movement.

Editorial scene cut

Short atmospheric clips that connect product, lifestyle, and brand feeling.

Social story rhythm

Vertical-ready moments for reels, stories, campaign previews, and launch teasers.

04 / palette

Muted, warm, tactile

The palette keeps the romance grounded: cream paper, berry reds, dusty rose, toasted orange, and soft shadow tones for editorial depth.
