DE SOI

This case study explores how De Soi can translate French aperitif culture, botanical wellness, and sober-curious commerce into a warm, cinematic shopping experience.

  • Web & Mobile

Overview

Lead with feeling. Support with proof. Convert with calm.

De Soi is not only selling a drink. It is selling permission to unwind differently. The experience needed to make a sober choice feel intentional, social, and desirable.

Ritual

Make the product feel like an evening before it feels like inventory.

Education

Explain adaptogens and flavor without becoming clinical.

Commerce

Keep product selection, pricing, and checkout direct.

My role

I shaped the ecommerce story from first impression to purchase path.

I focused on research synthesis, content hierarchy, visual pacing, product discovery, and interface modules that make desire and clarity work together.

  • Mapped the emotional and functional needs behind sober-curious shopping.
  • Structured the homepage around atmosphere, product discovery, proof, and conversion.
  • Designed a UI system where product color and sensory copy carry the brand feeling.
Problem

How might we make a sober choice feel indulgent and worth purchasing online?

Non-alcoholic products can be perceived as a compromise. De Soi needed to feel premium and desirable before asking the user to understand ingredients, formats, or subscription details.

Perception gap

The product needed to feel like pleasure, not restriction.

Education gap

Users need flavor, adaptogen, pack, and subscription clarity without friction.

Solutions

Design a purchase journey that feels sensory and composed.

  • Open with mood and ritual instead of a standard product grid.
  • Let users browse by flavor, color, occasion, and format.
  • Move functional benefits into supportive moments.
  • Keep cart, subscription, and pack selection familiar and easy.

Prototype
Statement

The final direction positions De Soi as a sensory ecommerce ritual: warm, botanical, premium, and clear enough to shop without losing its evening atmosphere.
