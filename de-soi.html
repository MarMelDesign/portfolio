My Role

As a Product Designer, I was responsible for designing and expanding key e-commerce experiences within the existing De Soi design system. My work focused on creating new PDP sections and designing the Subscription Popup Flow while ensuring full consistency with the established visual language, UX patterns, and brand guidelines.

Responsibilities

  • Designed new Product Detail Page (PDP) sections.
  • Designed the Subscription Popup Flow.
  • Applied and extended the existing design system.
  • Maintained consistency across typography, spacing, and component behavior.
  • Optimized user experience for subscription conversion.
  • Created responsive layouts for desktop and mobile.
  • Collaborated within established brand and product guidelines.
Project Brief

Project Context

De Soi already had a well-established visual identity, design language, and e-commerce ecosystem.

Rather than redesigning the experience from scratch, my role was to seamlessly integrate new content modules into the existing Product Detail Page while preserving the premium feel of the brand.

The challenge was ensuring that every new section felt native to the experience and supported both storytelling and purchase intent.

The Challenge

Balancing Storytelling and Conversion

Luxury beverage brands rely heavily on emotional storytelling, yet Product Detail Pages must also provide clear information that helps users make purchasing decisions.

The new PDP modules needed to:

  • Support product discovery
  • Reinforce brand value
  • Highlight ingredients and benefits
  • Improve content readability
  • Maintain visual consistency
  • Encourage purchase without disrupting the premium experience

Subscription Retention Flow

Overview

To reduce subscription cancellations and improve customer retention, I designed a multi-step cancellation flow that offers users alternative actions before fully ending their subscription.

The experience was built within the existing De Soi design system and focused on balancing user autonomy with retention opportunities.

The Challenge

Subscription cancellations directly impact recurring revenue.

The goal was to create a cancellation experience that:

  • Respects the user’s decision
  • Reduces unnecessary churn
  • Offers flexible alternatives
  • Maintains a premium brand experience
  • Keeps the flow simple and intuitive

Design Strategy

Instead of immediately cancelling a subscription, the flow introduces alternative actions that may better fit the customer’s needs.

Users can:

Pause Subscription

Temporarily skip upcoming shipments.

Change Delivery Frequency

Adjust shipment intervals based on consumption habits.

Change Products

Switch to a different product selection.

Change Flavor

Explore alternative flavor preferences.

Change Format

Move between available packaging formats.

Provide Cancellation Feedback

Help the business understand cancellation reasons.

Design Considerations

Reduce Friction

The experience needed to feel helpful rather than persuasive.

Preserve User Control

Users can continue with cancellation at any stage.

Encourage Retention Naturally

Alternative actions are presented as solutions, not obstacles.

Maintain Brand Consistency

Every modal follows the existing typography, spacing, color palette, and interaction patterns established across the De Soi ecosystem.

Business Impact

The flow transforms a cancellation action into a retention opportunity by offering customers flexible alternatives before they leave.

This approach helps:

  • Reduce subscription churn
  • Increase customer lifetime value
  • Improve user satisfaction
  • Gather valuable customer feedback
