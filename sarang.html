Mobile UX/UI case study

SARANG

SARANG is a Korean food delivery app concept focused on helping hungry users browse, choose, customize, and order with less friction.

  • 2025
  • Mobile App
  • Figma
  • Photoshop
Overview Role Problem Solutions Process Prototype Statement
Overview

Food UX has to respect appetite and time.

The project explores a warmer delivery experience where users can move from craving to checkout quickly while still enjoying the visual world of the food.

Speed

Reduce the number of decisions before cart.

Appetite

Use visuals and spacing to make dishes feel desirable.

Clarity

Keep pricing, timing, and checkout visible.

My role

I designed the ordering flow around quick decisions.

I focused on mobile hierarchy, card patterns, dish detail, and checkout clarity so the app feels easy to use when the user is hungry.

  • Structured browsing around categories, popular dishes, and fast actions.
  • Designed dish detail and cart modules for easy comparison.
  • Created a warm visual direction with mobile-friendly components.
Problem

Food delivery apps can make simple hunger feel like work.

Users need to scan food options quickly, understand details, and complete checkout without repeatedly hunting for price, timing, or customization.

Choice overload

Too many dishes and filters can slow the user down.

Checkout anxiety

Delivery time, address, and total need to be obvious before payment.

Solutions

Make ordering feel like a warm shortcut.

  • Lead with appetite through strong dish imagery.
  • Keep reorder, cart, and checkout actions close.
  • Make customization feel simple and reversible.
  • Use visual warmth without sacrificing mobile usability.
Process
Prototype
Statement

The final direction makes SARANG feel warm, direct, and easy to order from, with a mobile system that keeps food and action close together.
