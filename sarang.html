UX/UI case study · food delivery

SARANG

Making dinner choice feel quick, warm, and appetizing.

A Korean food delivery app concept focused on browsing, ordering, and making food feel emotionally close.

Choice overload

Food apps can overwhelm users with too many dishes and filters.

Speed

Hungry users need fast decisions and clear ordering steps.

Appetite

The UI needed to make food feel vivid and desirable.

Research

Research focused on how people choose food when they are hungry.

Visual trust

Food photography strongly affects confidence.

Menu logic

Categories, favorites, and popular dishes reduce decision time.

Checkout clarity

Delivery time, price, and address need to be obvious.

UX Strategy

Make ordering feel like a warm shortcut, not a task.

Lead with appetite

Use strong food visuals and simple categories.

Shorten the path

Keep reorder, cart, and checkout actions close.

Reduce hesitation

Show ratings, timing, dish details, and price early.

Wireframes

Wireframes tested browse, dish detail, cart, and checkout flow.

UI System

A warm mobile system built around appetite and speed.

Color

Warm reds and soft neutrals support appetite.

Cards

Dish cards show image, title, price, and quick actions.

Navigation

Bottom navigation keeps core actions reachable.

Homepage Breakdown

The home screen moves from craving to choice quickly.

Product Experience

The ordering experience keeps detail and action close together.

Dish detail

Image, description, options, and price stay easy to compare.

Cart

The cart confirms items, delivery, and total before checkout.

Motion

Motion makes ordering feel responsive and satisfying.

Final Outcome

A friendly delivery app designed for quick cravings.

The final direction makes SARANG warm, clear, and easy to order from.

Reflection

Food UX works best when it respects hunger.

Speed

Every extra step matters.

Emotion

Warm visuals make the app feel more human.
