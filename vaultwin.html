UX/UI case study

VAULTWIN

VAULTWIN translates a technical identity concept into a product experience built around trust, clear status, and controlled user action.

  • 2025
  • Web & Mobile
  • Figma
  • Photoshop
Overview Role Problem Solutions Process Prototype Statement
Overview

A technical product needs emotional confidence before explanation.

The product direction centers on making identity management feel stable, transparent, and safe without overwhelming users with blockchain language.

Security

Visual stability helps users trust the environment.

Clarity

Plain labels and visible states reduce uncertainty.

Control

Users should always know what is verified, pending, or active.

My role

I made the technical story more navigable.

I focused on UX structure, product hierarchy, and a restrained visual language that could support trust-heavy identity flows.

  • Organized identity actions into predictable product sequences.
  • Created a secure visual tone using depth, contrast, and rhythm.
  • Designed status-driven modules for verification and account states.
Problem

Blockchain identity can feel abstract before users understand its value.

The interface needed to reduce jargon, establish trust quickly, and make every identity state visible enough for users to feel in control.

Trust barrier

Users need confidence before interacting with identity systems.

Technical language

Blockchain concepts need translation into clear product behavior.

Solutions

Design confidence into the product path.

  • Show verification states and system feedback clearly.
  • Use plain language around complex identity actions.
  • Create a calm dashboard that supports high-trust decisions.
  • Make the brand feel futuristic without becoming cold.
Process
Prototype
Statement

The final direction frames VAULTWIN as a precise, controlled identity experience where users can understand system status and take action confidently.
