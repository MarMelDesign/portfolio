Overview

VaultWin is a blockchain-based platform that reimagines the traditional lottery experience through transparency, trust, and risk-conscious participation. Built on decentralized infrastructure, the platform combines Web3 technology with an intuitive, user-friendly interface to make complex blockchain interactions feel approachable and engaging. Instead of relying on opaque systems and uncertainty, VaultWin introduces a more transparent ecosystem where users can participate with confidence, track activity in real time, and understand exactly how the platform works.

My role

Role: End-to-End Product, Brand & Visual Designer

For Vaultwin, I led the entire creative and product design process from concept to final execution. I was responsible for creating the complete visual identity, including logo design, brand system, typography, color direction, and custom illustrations.

On the product side, I designed the full user experience and interface architecture, developing user flows, wireframes, information hierarchy, and responsive web design solutions tailored for a blockchain identity platform.

Problem and solutions

Project Problem

As blockchain ecosystems continue to grow, users face increasing challenges when managing their digital identities across multiple platforms and services. Existing solutions often feel overly technical, fragmented, and inaccessible to mainstream users.

The lack of trust, clarity, and intuitive user experiences creates barriers to adoption, making identity verification and digital asset management unnecessarily complex.

Vaultwin was created to bridge the gap between advanced blockchain technology and user-friendly digital experiences.

Project Goals

  • Create a trustworthy and recognizable brand identity for a blockchain-based platform.
  • Simplify complex blockchain identity management through intuitive UX design.
  • Build a clear information architecture that helps users understand and control their digital presence.
  • Increase user confidence through transparent interactions and consistent visual communication.
  • Design a scalable design system capable of supporting future platform growth and new features.
  • Deliver a modern and premium digital experience that appeals to both crypto-native and mainstream audiences.

Solutions

Solution 01 — Human-Centered UX

Complex blockchain processes were translated into simple, guided user flows that reduce friction and make identity management approachable for users of all experience levels.

Solution 02 — Unified Visual Ecosystem

A complete brand identity was developed, including logo, typography, color palette, and custom illustrations, creating a cohesive and memorable experience across all touchpoints.

Solution 03 — Trust Through Design

The interface utilizes clear hierarchy, structured layouts, and transparent interaction patterns to reinforce credibility and help users navigate sensitive identity-related actions with confidence.

Solution 04 — Scalable Design System

A flexible design framework was created to ensure consistency, maintainability, and future scalability as the platform evolves and expands.

Solution 05 — Modern Web3 Experience

By combining clean aesthetics with intuitive interactions, Vaultwin transforms a highly technical blockchain product into a modern, accessible, and engaging digital experience.

Process

Discovery & Research

The project began with exploring the current blockchain identity landscape and identifying common usability challenges users face when interacting with decentralized systems.

The research focused on understanding trust barriers, onboarding friction, identity verification flows, and how existing Web3 platforms communicate complex concepts to users.

This stage helped define the core opportunities for creating a more accessible and user-friendly experience.

Brand Strategy & Visual Direction

Before designing the product, a strong visual foundation was established.

The goal was to create a brand that feels secure, innovative, and trustworthy while remaining approachable to non-technical users.

Different visual directions, typography systems, color explorations, and identity concepts were evaluated before defining the final design language.

Illustration System

Custom illustrations were created to support the brand identity and simplify communication of complex blockchain concepts through approachable visual storytelling.

Design Solutions

Wireframing

Low-fidelity wireframes were created to establish layout structure, content hierarchy, and user interaction patterns before moving into visual design.

High-Fidelity UI Design

The wireframes evolved into polished interfaces with a strong focus on clarity, accessibility, visual hierarchy, and modern Web3 aesthetics.

Statement

The final product successfully combines blockchain technology with a user-centered design approach, making participation simple, transparent, and enjoyable for both experienced crypto users and newcomers.

🏆 The project and its design were recognized with the Best Bahamut Game Award, highlighting both the product vision and the quality of the overall user experience.
